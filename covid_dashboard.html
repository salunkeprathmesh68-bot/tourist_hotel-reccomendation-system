Verified Mathematical & Epidemiological Formulas

Exact formulas implemented in Python (Pandas & NumPy) for verified reproducible analytics

1. Active Infected Cases
Active = Confirmed - (Cured + Deaths)

Computes the real-time clinical burden on healthcare facilities and hospital beds.

2. Clinical Recovery Rate (%)
Recovery Rate = (Recovered ÷ Confirmed) × 100

Reflects patient cure efficiency, therapeutic protocols, and public health resilience.

3. Case Fatality Rate (CFR %)
CFR % = (Total Deaths ÷ Confirmed) × 100

Measures the proportion of fatalities among identified cases (India averaged ~1.37%).

Lab Viva Voce Exam Q&A (परीक्षेसाठी महत्त्वाचे प्रश्न)

Quick answers in English with Marathi explanations for viva oral presentation

Q1: Which libraries were used in this project?

Ans: Pandas (data manipulation & cleaning), NumPy (numerical calculations), Matplotlib & Seaborn (chart generation), and Chart.js for web visuals.

मराठी: डेटा क्लीनिंगसाठी Pandas आणि चार्ट्ससाठी Matplotlib, Seaborn व Chart.js वापरले आहे.

Q2: How was Data Cleaning performed?

Ans: Missing numeric values were identified using df.isnull().sum() and filled with 0 using fillna(0). Duplicates removed with drop_duplicates(), and dates parsed using to_datetime().

मराठी: missing व्हॅल्यूज fillna(0) ने भरल्या आणि drop_duplicates() ने रिपीट नोंदी काढल्या.

Q3: Why use a Dual Y-Axis in Visual 3?

Ans: Recoveries are in millions (34M) while deaths are in thousands (472K). A single axis flattens the death curve, so a secondary axis ensures clear dual scaling.

मराठी: मृत्यू हजारात आणि रिकव्हरी कोटीत असल्याने मृत्यूची रेषा चपटी होऊ नये म्हणून दुहेरी axis वापरला.

Q4: What was the peak epidemic period?

Ans: May 2021 was the Second Wave peak with over 5.2 Million new cases added in one month. Wave 1 peaked in September-October 2020.

मराठी: मे २०२१ मध्ये दुसरी लाट शिखरावर होती ज्यामध्ये एका महिन्यात ५२ लाख रुग्ण वाढले होते.
